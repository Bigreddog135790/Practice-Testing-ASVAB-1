What is the AFQT?

Your AFQT score is a percentile from 1 to 99 — it says how you did compared to everyone else who took the test. It comes from only four of the ASVAB sections:

AR — Arithmetic Reasoningmath word problems
MK — Mathematics Knowledgealgebra & geometry
WK — Word Knowledgevocabulary
PC — Paragraph Comprehensionreading

The AFQT decides if you can enlist at all. The other ASVAB sections decide which jobs you qualify for. Minimum AFQT scores run roughly 31–36 for most branches and around 40 for the Coast Guard — those change over time, so confirm the current number with a recruiter.
